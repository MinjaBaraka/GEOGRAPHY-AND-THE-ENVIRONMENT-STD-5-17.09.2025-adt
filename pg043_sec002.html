Activity 1

Observe the population distribution in your village or street, and then give a summary describing the type and reasons for the population distribution you observed.

Factors affecting population distribution

Population distribution results from various factors. These can be geographical factors such as climate, relief, soil fertility, availability of water resources, and minerals or human factors like infrastructure, social services, politics, business, and the growth of technology and industries. All these factors are important in making decisions about where to live.
