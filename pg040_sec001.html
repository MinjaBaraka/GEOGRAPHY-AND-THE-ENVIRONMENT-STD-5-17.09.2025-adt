Geography and the Environment Pupil’s Book Standard Five

Human resources
people’s skills, knowledge, and labour
Infrastructure
physical systems like roads, buildings, and electricity
Irrigation
the supply of water to crops to help growth, typically by means of channels
National park
a large area of natural land protected for conservation and tourism
Rift Valley Lake
a lake formed due to tectonic activity in the East African Rift Valley
Saline lake
a lake with high salt content
Sustainable use
using resources wisely to ensure they last
Tributary
a smaller river or stream that flows into a larger one
