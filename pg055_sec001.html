Weather
Introduction
Understanding the elements of weather is essential in our daily lives. Weather affects nearly everything we do. This includes choosing what to wear, planning a trip, or carrying out various production activities. Therefore, people must develop an understanding of the weather. In this chapter, you will learn the concept of weather and how to measure and record weather elements. The competencies developed will enable you to adopt a lifestyle that adapts to changes in weather.

Meaning of weather
Weather refers to the atmospheric conditions of a specific area recorded over a short period, usually hours or a few days. For example, the atmosphere may be cloudy, sunny or rainy on a particular day. Weather affects the activities we do and the type of clothes we wear. For instance, we wear light clothes on hot, sunny days. On cold days, we wear coats and sweaters, or raincoats during rainy conditions.
Weather is measured and recorded at specific centres called weather stations. The study of the atmosphere and weather is known as meteorology. A person who is knowledgeable about weather is called a meteorologist or weather expert.
