Minerals

Minerals are valuable resources extracted from the ground. Areas rich in minerals often provide various opportunities due to mining activities. These opportunities include jobs in mines, businesses related to food, clothing, accommodation, entertainment places, and financial services. There are also transportation opportunities to serve the workers, other residents, and to transport various goods. People are attracted to live in areas with these resources to take advantage of the available opportunities. Additionally, people are attracted to these places because they experience rapid development, such as roads, schools, and hospitals.

Human Factors

Human factors refer to social, economic, political, and cultural aspects that influence the distribution of people in various areas. Some of these factors include the following:

Infrastructure

Infrastructure such as roads, bridges, communication systems and electricity influence people’s selection of where to live. Areas with good infrastructure like Dar es Salaam, attract many people because they are easily accessible. Furthermore, such infrastructure facilitates business and improves the quality of life. As a result, this situation causes an increase in the population in these areas.

Social Services

The availability of social services such as healthcare, education and housing affects the distribution of people within wards, districts or regions. For example, in Tanzania, urban areas have better social services compared to rural areas. More people choose to move from rural areas to establish homes in urban areas to be closer to quality healthcare and education for their children. For instance, the presence of referral hospitals and universities in large cities attracts people from other areas, leading to a higher population in urban areas.

Political decisions and government policies

Political decision and government policies can significantly affect the distribution of people. For example, the decision of the government to shift headquarters to the city of Dodoma has led to infrastructure development and has increased the population of that city.
