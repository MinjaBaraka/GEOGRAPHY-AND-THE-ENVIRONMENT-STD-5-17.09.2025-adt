Exercise 2

Answer the following questions:

1. Explain the effects resulting from a large population on the environment.

2. How can communities conserve green spaces?

3. Explain how the government can manage environmental conservation in large cities like Dar es Salaam and Mwanza.

4. Why is it easier to manage the environment in areas with sparse population or uniform population distribution?

5. Using the saying ‘Take care of the environment and the environment will take care of you’, write an essay to encourage people living in densely populated areas to conserve the environment.
