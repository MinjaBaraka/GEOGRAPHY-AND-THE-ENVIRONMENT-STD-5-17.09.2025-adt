Mining

Mining involves the extraction of valuable materials found underground. Usually, it is carried out using machines or manually with simple tools like hoes and pickaxes. Changes in weather can have a major impact on the efficiency, safety, and well-being of miners. Heavy rains can block mining channels and cause flooding, making the environment dangerous for miners, such as landslides that can damage machinery and equipment, leading to accidents or fatalities.

Drought reduces water availability and hardens the ground for mining, making mining more difficult and reducing the quality of mineral processing. High temperature can lead to health issues for miners. Strong winds increase dust levels. This leads to respiratory diseases such as severe colds, and tuberculosis (TB), which affect miners’ health. Therefore, miners should use specialised equipment to predict weather or monitor hourly weather forecasts. This will help them determine when it is safe to carry out mining activities.
