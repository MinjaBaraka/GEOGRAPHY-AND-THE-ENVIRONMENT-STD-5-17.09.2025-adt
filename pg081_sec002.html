Fishing

Fishing is a production activity that involves the harvesting of products from the sea, lakes, rivers, and ponds using special tools such as nets and hooks. In the past, this activity was carried out mainly for obtaining food. Over time, fishing has become a source of income like other occupations. This is due to the high demand for fish and the availability of modern fishing equipment such as advanced boats. Weather is important in fishing activities. Cool weather, for example, allows fishing to take place, while strong winds and heavy rains can make fishing difficult and dangerous. In addition, fishing is mostly done at night, early morning, or in the evening because the darkness encourages fish to gather near the water surface in large numbers, making them easier to catch. However, when there is sunlight or moonlight, most fish tend to hide, making it harder for fishermen to catch them easily.
