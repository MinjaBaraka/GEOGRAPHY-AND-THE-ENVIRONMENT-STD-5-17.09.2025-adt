However, good weather increases crop production, leading to higher and better yields and better prices for farmers. Increased production also lowers food costs to consumers and boosts input-related businesses such as fertilisers and seeds. To maximise benefits, traders should monitor weather forecasts.

Good weather, such as rain, sunshine, and moderate temperature, attract many tourists. This increases income for hotel owners, tour guides, and tourism-related businesses. These businesses include selling clothes, carvings, scenic photos, and various souvenirs. Good weather also enables tour guides to provide better services. However, heavy rains or storms can lead to a decrease in the number of tourists, damage tourism infrastructure, disrupt transportation, and cause wildlife to hide. This affects tourism activities, reduces income and profits, and increases operational costs for tourism activities.

In the transportation of goods, adverse weather such as strong winds and heavy rain delay the movement of goods via roads, railways, seas, and air. Floods caused by heavy rain can make roads impassable, disrupt train schedules, and even cause transportation accidents. This situation increases transportation costs as traders are forced to use alternative routes or wait for the weather to improve.
