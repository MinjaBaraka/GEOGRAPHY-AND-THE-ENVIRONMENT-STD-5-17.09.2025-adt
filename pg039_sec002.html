Vocabulary

Blue economy
economic activities linked to water resources
Biodiversity
a variety of living organisms in an area
Capital resources
tools, machines, and infrastructure used for production
Delta
a landform at the mouth of a river which is created by sediment deposits
Export
selling goods to other countries
Game reserve
a protected area for wildlife where regulated hunting may be allowed
Gemstones
precious stones used in jewellery
Hydropower
electricity which is generated using moving water
