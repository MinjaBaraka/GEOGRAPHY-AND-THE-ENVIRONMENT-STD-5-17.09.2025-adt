Steps for measuring temperature using a maximum thermometer

(a)
Place the maximum thermometer outside where it can sense the day’s temperature, for instance, under a shaded area, but not in direct sunlight.
(b)
Wait for the temperature to rise, as the temperature increases, the mercury inside the thermometer expands and rises in the glass tube.
(c)
By the evening, take the thermometer and read the highest temperature by checking where the mercury has reached. Note that the highest point where the index has stopped indicates the maximum temperature of that day.
(d)
Record the number closest to the index line to show the maximum temperature of the day.

Steps for measuring temperature using a minimum thermometer

(a)
Place the minimum thermometer outside, in a cool area, close to the ground, to measure the minimum temperature (cold).
(b)
Wait for the temperature to drop. As it gets colder during the night or early morning, the alcohol inside the thermometer will fall down the glass tube.
(c)
In the early morning, before the sun rises, check where the alcohol has moved to. The point where the index stops shows the lowest temperature of the day.
(d)
Record the number closest to where the index has stopped to show the coldest or minimum temperature of the day.
