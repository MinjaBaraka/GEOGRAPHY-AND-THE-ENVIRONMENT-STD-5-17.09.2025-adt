Introduction

This textbook, Geography and the Environment for Primary School, is written based on the Geography and the Environment syllabus for primary Education Standards III-VI, issued by the Ministry of Education, Science and Technology in 2023. Some of the contents in this textbook has been extracted from the Social Studies textbooks for Standards Four, Five, Six, and Seven, published in 2018 based on the 2016 syllabus for Primary Education Standards III-VII. This textbook contains four chapters: The natural resources of Tanzania, Population distribution and the use of natural resources, Weather, and The relationship between weather and production activities. By learning the contents of this textbook, you will develop the knowledge, skills and attitude in using and conserving natural resources of Tanzania and engaging in production activities following the prevailing weather.

The contents of this textbook is presented in the form of text, activities, maps, figures and exercises to enable you develop the intended competencies in each chapter. Each chapter has sufficient exercises to test your understanding of the specific themes. Therefore, you are advised to do all the exercises and activities in this textbook, along with any additional activities provided by the teacher, to develop the intended competencies. Additional learning resources are available in the TIE library, at https://ol.tie.go.tz or ol.tie.go.tz
