Bad weather

Consumers
individuals or groups who purchase and use goods and services
Drought
a long period when there is little or no rain
Durable materials
materials that are strong and capable of withstanding stress, wear, and adverse conditions
Eco-friendly
something that is not harmful to the environment.
Miners
people who work to extract minerals, from underground or surface
Pasture

Production activities
Structured processes of producing goods or provision of services
