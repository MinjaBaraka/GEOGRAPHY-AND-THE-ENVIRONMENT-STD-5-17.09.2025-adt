Geography and the Environment Pupil’s Book Standard Five

Activity 2

Follow the following steps, and then answer the following questions:

1. Take two containers of equal volume.
2. Fill both containers with the same amount of water.
3. Place one container in a location that does not get direct sunlight.
4. Place the second container outside where there is direct sunlight.
5. Leave both containers undisturbed for four hours.
(a) After four hours measure the temperature of the water in both containers using a thermometer.
Compare which container has a higher water temperature than the other.
(b) Give a short report of the results from your experiment using the following points:
(i) The difference in the water temperatures you measured; and
(ii) The reasons for the difference in the water temperatures you measured.
