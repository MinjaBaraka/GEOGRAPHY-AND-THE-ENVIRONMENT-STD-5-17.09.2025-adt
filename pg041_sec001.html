Chapter Two

Population distribution and the use of natural resources

Introduction

Population distribution can either promote or hinder the sustainable use of natural resources. As the population increases and spreads across various areas of Tanzania, the demand for natural resources such as water, land and energy also rises. In this chapter, you will learn about the meaning and types of population distribution, the geographical and human factors of population distribution, and the relationship between population distribution and the use of natural resources. Additionally, you will learn about the impacts of population distribution on the environment. The competencies developed will enable you to conserve natural resources for sustainable use.
