Activity 8

Prepare an empty plastic bottle, two bowls, a balloon, warm water, and cold water.

(a) Pour warm water into one bowl and cold water into another.
(b) Place the balloon over the mouth of the bottle.
(c) Put the bottle in the warm water and observe what happens.
(d) Then, place the bottle in the cold water and observe what happens.

Explain why the balloon expands or shrinks after being placed in warm or cold water.
