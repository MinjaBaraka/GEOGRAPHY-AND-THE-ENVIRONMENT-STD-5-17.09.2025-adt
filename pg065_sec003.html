Measuring atmospheric pressure

Atmospheric pressure is measured in millibars using an instrument called barometer. There are three main types of barometers: mercury barometers, aneroid barometers, and digital barometers. All three types serve the same function of recording atmospheric pressure. A mercury barometer is a long glass tube with a measuring scale.
