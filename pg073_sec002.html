Geography and the Environment Pupil’s Book Standard Five

Activity 10

Adapted Activity: Observe your surroundings using your senses, then identify the current weather conditions by noting:

(a) The warmth or coolness of the air.

(b) Whether you feel sunlight directly on your skin.

(c) Whether you feel moisture, mist, drizzle, or rain.

Suggest the amount of cloud cover and the type of cloud based on the weather conditions you feel. After that, predict the expected weather based on the type of cloud you identified.
