Business and economic opportunities

Areas with many business opportunities attract many people due to employment and investment opportunities. In Tanzania for example, Dar es Salaam city is a major business hub where jobs are available in sectors like trade, manufacturing and services. For example, the Dar es Salaam Port is the largest in East Africa and serves as a key gateway for international trade. Thus, attracting businesses and investors.

Technological development

Technological development leads to an increase in the population in some areas. For example, digital technology, the internet, and mobile phone networks have attracted many people to live in areas where these services are available. Additionally, improvements in mobile financial transaction services offered by different mobile network companies have facilitated easy and quick access to financial services. This encourages significant business activity and attracts people to live in these areas in search of business opportunities.

Industrial development

Industrial development and companies are among the factors that influence the distribution of people. The development of industries in Tanzania has led many people to move to areas with factories in search of employment and business opportunities. For example, Dar es Salaam, Dodoma, Tanga, Mwanza, Mbeya, and Arusha regions attract more people due to the availability of various industries such as agriculture and food processing, fisheries, manufacturing, shipping and mineral procesing industries.
