The Julius Nyerere Hydropower station

The Julius Nyerere Hydropower Project (JNHPP) is the largest hydropower dam project for Tanzania, aiming to meet the growing energy demands and reduce power shortages in the country. It is located at Stiegler’s Gorge on the Rufiji River. The dam creates a water reservoir with a storage capacity of about 34 billion cubic metres, covering an area of approximately 1,200 square kilometres. The dam also helps in the control of floods.
